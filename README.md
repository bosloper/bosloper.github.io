# Bosloper — Roaming the Bush

*"He who roams the bush"*

Welcome! This site is a personal record of my journeys through wild places —
shared with fellow naturalists, researchers, and conservation stakeholders.

## What you will find here

- **Trip reports & narratives** — field observations from the bush
- **Biodiversity data** — species lists and distribution notes
- **Swadini Biodiversity Project** — research reports and dashboards
- **Photos** — wildlife and habitat imagery from the field

## About Bosloper

I am a student of biodiversity, bio-mapping advisor, and citizen scientist,
with a particular focus on the Swadini area of the Blyde River Canyon region.
The biodiversity reports and dashboards on this site were developed in
collaboration with Claude AI.

## Contact & Collaboration

If you are a researcher, conservationist, or stakeholder interested in the
Swadini data, feel free to reach out via GitHub.
